## 1. Purpose

This vault is a **second brain for building defensible expertise as an analytics engineer**, aimed at an independent consulting practice that serves clients across industries.

It is **not** a generic notes app. It implements the LLM Wiki pattern: a persistent, compounding synthesis that Claude maintains on my behalf. I curate sources and ask questions. Claude does the bookkeeping that makes knowledge accumulate.

**The moat is analytics-engineering craft and judgment that travels across domains**, not depth in any single vertical. Generalists compete on price. This vault exists so I compete on craft and demonstrable range.

**Defensibility comes from three things, in this order:**
1. **Craft depth:** data modeling, transformation, testing, orchestration and data quality, plus the judgment to apply them well. This is the centerpiece.
2. **Opinionated positions:** my own frameworks and takes (e.g. "model for the question, not the table"), refined over time.
3. **Receipts across domains:** anonymized case studies from real engagements that prove the craft travels.

Domain knowledge (finance, e-commerce, healthcare…) is a first-class but **pluggable** layer. A domain note earns its place when it connects back to a transferable craft lesson.
