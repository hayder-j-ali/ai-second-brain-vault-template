# Example: analytics engineer building an independent practice

A worked configuration showing what the `{{…}}` placeholders look like once they're filled in, for an analytics engineer building a consulting practice. Copy what fits, then rewrite it in your own words. The value of the vault comes from §1 describing *your* goals.

- `claude-md-section-1.md` is a filled-in CLAUDE.md §1 (Purpose).
- `digest-config.md` is a filled-in ⚙️ configuration section for `_meta/digest-spec.md`.

Other fields map onto the same shape. The core craft becomes your discipline's methods, and the demand signals become whatever your clients or employers are paying for.

| Field | Core craft | Demand signals |
|---|---|---|
| Product design | research, interaction design, design systems | agency RFPs, contract rates |
| Employment law | case-law analysis, drafting | regulatory changes driving client work |
| DevOps / SRE | reliability engineering, IaC | on-call and platform-migration contracts |
